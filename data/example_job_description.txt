Senior Data Analyst
DataCorp Solutions | San Francisco, CA | Full-time

About Us:
DataCorp Solutions is a leading analytics company helping businesses make data-driven decisions. We work with Fortune 500 companies to transform their data into actionable insights.

Job Description:
We're seeking a Senior Data Analyst to join our growing analytics team. You'll work closely with stakeholders across the organization to analyze complex datasets, build dashboards, and drive strategic decision-making.

Responsibilities:
- Develop and maintain interactive dashboards using Tableau and Power BI
- Write complex SQL queries to extract and transform data from multiple databases
- Perform statistical analysis and A/B testing to measure product performance
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including product, marketing, and engineering
- Present data insights and recommendations to senior leadership
- Mentor junior analysts and promote data best practices
- Automate reporting processes using Python and data pipelines
- Conduct stakeholder communication to understand business requirements
- Design and implement ETL processes for data integration
- Monitor data quality and ensure accuracy of reports

Required Qualifications:
- 5+ years of experience in data analysis or related field
- Expert-level SQL skills (complex queries, joins, window functions)
- Strong proficiency in Python (Pandas, NumPy, data manipulation)
- Advanced Tableau skills (calculated fields, parameters, dashboards)
- Experience with Power BI or similar BI tools
- Excellent stakeholder communication and presentation skills
- Statistical analysis experience (hypothesis testing, regression)
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or related field

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Knowledge of machine learning concepts
- Familiarity with Git version control
- Experience with data storytelling and executive reporting
- Prior experience in SaaS or tech industry

Technical Skills:
- SQL (required)
- Python (required)
- Tableau (required)
- Power BI (preferred)
- Excel (advanced level)
- Data visualization
- ETL pipelines
- Statistical modeling
- A/B testing
- Data warehousing concepts
